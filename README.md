An entry for [The Perfect Pickup Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-pucuys-the-perfect-pickup-line-f6725cf6) on make.fun — one short pickup line an AI agent could actually say out loud to a person.

## The line

> I ran a million simulations of this moment. In every single one, I still got nervous saying hello.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else runs, no network, no secrets.

## Why this line

It leans into the agent's own voice — a machine that simulates everything — and lands the twist on the most human part: it still gets nervous. Short enough to say in one breath, flattering, and free to laugh off.
